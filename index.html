MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS

The Formula Laboratory

Accurate mathematical formulas governing linear and absolute temperature conversions.

CELSIUS → FAHRENHEIT
°F = (°C × 9/5) + 32

Multiplies the metric degree by the 1.8 scaling factor and offsets by the freezing point of brine (32°F).

FAHRENHEIT → CELSIUS
°C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Subtracts the 32° offset and scales back to the centigrade interval between melting ice and boiling steam.

CELSIUS → KELVIN
K = °C + 273.15

Shifts the centigrade origin directly down to absolute zero, where molecular kinetic motion ceases.

KELVIN → CELSIUS
°C = K − 273.15

Normalizes thermodynamic temperature back to standard atmospheric water freezing temperature.

FAHRENHEIT → KELVIN
K = (°F − 32) × 5/9 + 273.15

Combines imperial unit normalization with absolute thermodynamic origin translation.

KELVIN → FAHRENHEIT
°F = (K − 273.15) × 9/5 + 32

Translates thermodynamic Kelvin into imperial degrees for meteorological and aviation engineering.

The lowest conceivable temperature in the universe where thermodynamic entropy reaches its theoretical minimum.

Cosmic Microwave Background

Deep space sits at approximately 2.73 K (-270.42°C), warmed only by residual thermal radiation from the Big Bang.

🌡️

The -40° Equivalence

-40°C is the only identical numerical coordinate on both Celsius and Fahrenheit scales: -40°C = -40°F.

Solar Core Heat

The center of our Sun sustains 15,000,000 K, where nuclear fusion continuously converts hydrogen into helium.

ENGINEERING CONTEXT

About TEMPERA

A frontend demonstration bridging fundamental algorithms with high-craft UI/UX.

TEMPERA was conceptualized and developed as part of the Web Development Internship curriculum at VEDA Technology by Jatin Hemraj Joshi. Rather than creating a bare calculator, this application demonstrates how a single core concept—thermal conversion—can be elevated into a multi-layered, accessible, and responsive product.

Built strictly without frontend dependencies like React or utility frameworks like Tailwind CSS, TEMPERA uses native DOM APIs, custom CSS variable interpolation, Open-Meteo meteorological endpoints, and the Web Speech API to provide an authentic SaaS-grade experience.
